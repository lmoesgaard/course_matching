Scores are cosine similarities between TF‑IDF vectors of each course's official description, bounded 0–1. They measure vocabulary overlap, not learning outcomes or credit equivalence — treat a high score as a prompt to read both descriptions, never as a merit decision. Danish and English descriptions are compared as written, so a course taught in one language may score low against a close counterpart taught in the other.
